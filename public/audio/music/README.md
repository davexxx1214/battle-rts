# Ironfield outcome music

These MP3 files were supplied in the project's `music` directory and copied here for browser playback.

## Outcome mapping

- Victory: `victory1.mp3`, `victory2.mp3`, `victory3.mp3` — one track is selected randomly and played once when the player wins.
- Defeat or draw: `fail.mp3` — played once when the player loses or the battle ends in a draw.

There is no background music. Outcome music and UI sound effects share one master toggle. Playback is enabled by default, and every final gain is multiplied by `0.5`.
